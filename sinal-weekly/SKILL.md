# The week, in two lines

```
sinal summary --days 7 --json
```

Write at most two sentences to the owner, in his language. Say the count first,
then the one thing that stands out, and nothing else:

> 7 of 7 mornings answered, usually within 9 minutes. One pause on Thursday, for
> the trip to your sister's.

When a morning escalated, name what happened and who closed it, with the
minutes: that number is the only proof this thing works. When nothing happened,
say so in one line and stop. Never pad it with encouragement, and never add
advice about her health or her routine.
